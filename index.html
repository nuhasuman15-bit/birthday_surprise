Relationship date - 14 May 2020

14 May 2020 — The day our story began ❤️

We met on 14 May 2020, during Ramadan. I still remember that time so clearly. Somehow, in the middle of such a difficult and lonely period, Allah brought you into my life, and that became one of the happiest things that happened to me. Honestly, I was feeling very low and lonely at that time. Lockdown had made everything even harder, and I had no one to really talk to. I used to make a dua to Allah that someday He would send someone into my life who would truly love me and stay by my side. And then you came into my life. ❤️ I will never forget that evening. It was around 7 PM when you messaged me at 7:03, and I replied at 7:05. 🥹 I never knew that such a simple message would become the beginning of something so beautiful. I am genuinely so grateful that you came into my life. Thank you for finding me when I was feeling so alone. Thank you for staying with me, loving me, and being a part of my life. Sometimes I think about what my life would have been like if you had never come, or if you had never proposed to me. Maybe things would have been completely different. But Allah knew exactly what I needed, and He sent you to me at a time when I needed someone the most. ❤️ I can say “thank you” a thousand times, and it still wouldn't be enough. And no matter how much I say Alhamdulillah and thank Allah for bringing you into my life, it will never feel like enough. That evening, that message, and the moment you came into my life… I will never forget them. ❤️
